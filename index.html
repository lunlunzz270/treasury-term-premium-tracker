Empirical Research & Cross-Market Transmission

Benchmark Event Window • July 14, 2023 to October 19, 2023

Over this specific 3-month window, the 10-Year ACM Treasury Term Premium experienced its most aggressive surge in over a decade, advancing from -0.52% (-52 bps) to a peak of +0.33% (+33 bps)—a net jump of +85 basis points.

Transmission to Equities (S&P 500 Index):
During this exact duration re-pricing window, the U.S. Equity Market suffered a textbook technical correction. The S&P 500 peaked on July 31, 2023 at 4,588.96 and declined to a low of 4,117.37 on October 27, 2023, representing a net pullback of -10.28% (-471.59 points).

Economic Mechanism:
The term premium represents the compensation investors require for holding duration risk rather than rolling over short-term bills. In the second half of 2023, the U.S. Treasury's unexpected increase in coupon auction sizes (the Quarterly Refunding Announcement) led investors to demand significantly higher compensation to absorb duration. This +85 bps term premium surge accounted for 72.6% of the total 117 bps increase in 10-year Treasury yields (3.81% to 4.98%). Because the 10-year yield anchors the discount rate in corporate valuation models, this jump compressed the S&P 500 forward P/E multiple from 20.4x down to 18.2x.
